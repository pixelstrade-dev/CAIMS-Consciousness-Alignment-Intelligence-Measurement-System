# Support

- **Bug or unexpected score behavior**: open a [bug report](https://github.com/pixelstrade-dev/CAIMS-Consciousness-Alignment-Intelligence-Measurement-System/issues/new?template=bug_report.md). Include the `metadata` block of the score (it carries protocol version, prompt hash and provider — that is what makes your report reproducible).
- **Feature ideas**: [feature request](https://github.com/pixelstrade-dev/CAIMS-Consciousness-Alignment-Intelligence-Measurement-System/issues/new?template=feature_request.md).
- **Scientific critique or falsification results**: use the [research template](https://github.com/pixelstrade-dev/CAIMS-Consciousness-Alignment-Intelligence-Measurement-System/issues/new?template=research.md) — negative results are welcome and get published.
- **Questions & discussion**: [Discord](https://discord.gg/XqmDkPdu6h).
- **Security vulnerabilities**: do NOT open a public issue — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
